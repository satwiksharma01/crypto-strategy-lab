    HOW THIS LAB WORKS

    Know what you’re testing.

    The assumptions are part of the result.

    01

    Explicit, supported rules

    The first release recognizes moving average crossovers, RSI entry and exit thresholds, and buy-and-hold for BTC / USDT and ETH / USDT. Choose the free parser, connect your own Gemini key, or exchange a task with your local agent. AI and agents propose rules; the app validates their format. Compare the rules with your original request before every run because a model can omit or misinterpret a condition. No generated code is executed.

    02

    Historical data with a fingerprint

    Tests use the bundled daily candle datasets. The Evidence tab records the source, date range, and SHA-256 fingerprint of the actual dataset. Changing data can change results; a remix link carries settings and requires a fresh run.

    03

    Signals now. Execution next bar.

    Indicators use completed daily closes. A signal fills at the next available daily open. Fees apply to each side; slippage moves the execution price against the trade. Positions are long only, use the selected fraction of cash, and close at the final test close. No leverage, shorts, or perpetual funding.

    04

    Count the costs and the losses

    Returns and trade wins are measured after fees. Drawdown measures the largest decline from a prior equity peak. Buy-and-hold provides a comparison with the same trading costs and capital allocation. A short period or a small number of trades provides limited evidence.

    05

    A test, not a forecast

    The simulation cannot reproduce order book liquidity, outages, market impact, taxes, or your actual fills. Trying many settings on the same period can overfit the past. This version does not optimize parameters, connect a wallet, place orders, or establish that a strategy will be profitable.
